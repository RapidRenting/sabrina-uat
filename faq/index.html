Frequently asked questions

A few practical details to help you feel prepared before your appointment.

What should I book for my first appointment?

Please choose an initial appointment when booking your first visit with Sabrina. Jane lists the current appointment options and availability.

Do I need a doctor’s referral?

No. You don’t need a referral to see Sabrina. Some insurance plans require one for reimbursement, so please check with your insurance provider.

Does Sabrina treat minors?

Yes. Clients 16 and older can attend on their own, as long as they understand the proposed treatment and can give informed consent. Clients under 16 should come to their appointment with a parent or guardian.

What happens during an initial appointment?

Your initial appointment includes a review of your health history, a focused assessment, collaborative treatment planning, informed consent and hands-on treatment. These steps all take place within the time you booked.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Wear whatever is comfortable for you. You’re welcome to wear shorts during treatment, and Sabrina uses draping so you stay covered at all times. Many techniques work best directly on the skin, but treatment can also be done over clothing or over the sheet if you prefer. If you’re coming in with an injury, Sabrina will do an assessment, so wear comfortable clothing you can move in. Depending on your needs, she may also suggest remedial exercises. Please bring water. Before your first appointment, complete your health history in Jane; otherwise it will need to be done during your appointment, which takes time away from treatment. There’s no need to arrive early. Sabrina keeps the space as scent-free as possible, so please wear little or no scented products.

Can I choose what’s treated or change the pressure?

Yes. You’re in control of your treatment. Let Sabrina know which areas you’d like her to focus on or avoid, and speak up at any time if you’d like the pressure changed. You can also ask her to modify or stop treatment at any point.

Do you offer pregnancy massage?

Yes. Sabrina offers pregnancy massage with you lying on your side, supported by pillows. If you’re pregnant, please let her know through Jane, in your appointment note or your health history form.

Does Sabrina offer TMJ treatment?

Treatment may include work to the muscles around the jaw and temporomandibular joint (TMJ) when it is appropriate for your needs. Sabrina will explain the proposed approach and obtain your informed consent before treatment.

When can I book with Sabrina?

Sabrina is at Gladstone on Mondays and Tuesdays from 8 a.m. to 3 p.m., Wednesdays from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., Thursdays from noon to 7 p.m., Fridays from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. and Sundays from 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. Jane is the source of truth for live availability.

Where is Sabrina’s location, and what is the parking situation?

Sabrina is at Unit 1, 481 Gladstone Avenue. The entrance is around the back of the building, so please walk around to the back rather than using the front door. Free two-hour street parking is available on Gladstone and nearby side streets, and there is a designated waiting area.

Is the location accessible?

The Gladstone location does not currently have step-free access. The entrance is around the back of the building, and there are steps at the back entrance. A washroom is available for clients, but it is not accessible. If you have questions about access before booking, email massage@sabrinathermt.com.

How do I book or change an appointment?

Use Sabrina’s secure Jane booking site to book or manage a 45-, 60- or 90-minute appointment. 30-minute appointments are available upon request only and cannot be booked online; email massage@sabrinathermt.com to request one. Visit the Services & pricing page for appointment fees. Current policies and live availability are maintained in Jane.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please give at least 48 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Cancellations made with less than 48 hours’ notice and missed appointments are charged the full appointment fee. You can cancel online through Jane. To reschedule, or if you need help rebooking, email massage@sabrinathermt.com. Email is the preferred way to reach Sabrina; please allow 1 to 2 business days for a reply. If you are running late, call or text 613-801-6160.

What payment methods do you accept?

In person, Sabrina accepts cash, debit and credit cards (except American Express). Online through Jane, you can pay by credit card, including American Express; debit and cash aren’t available online. American Express is accepted only through Jane, so add the card to your Jane profile to use it.

Do you offer gift cards or gift certificates?

Yes. You can buy a gift card online on Jane’s gift card page, where amounts match Sabrina’s appointment fees (you’ll need to sign in or create a Jane account). For a different amount, up to $500, email massage@sabrinathermt.com or ask in person.

Do you provide insurance receipts or direct billing?

Sabrina does not direct bill insurance. Jane automatically emails you a receipt that you can submit to your insurance provider, and a printed receipt is available on request. Massage therapy coverage varies by plan, so please check with your insurance provider about your coverage and whether a doctor’s referral is required.

How can I contact Sabrina?

Email is the preferred way to reach Sabrina, including for booking questions, rescheduling and appointment requests: massage@sabrinathermt.com. Please allow 1 to 2 business days for a reply. If you are running late for an appointment, call or text 613-801-6160. Please do not include personal health information in email, text messages or voicemail. Use Jane to book, cancel or manage standard appointments and to enter health-history information.

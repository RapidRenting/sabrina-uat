Frequently asked questions

A few practical details to help you feel prepared before your appointment.

What should I book for my first appointment?

Please choose an initial appointment when booking your first visit with me. Jane lists the current appointment options and availability.

Do I need a doctor’s referral?

No. You don’t need a referral to see me. Some insurance plans require one for reimbursement, so please check with your insurance provider.

Do you treat minors?

Yes. Clients 16 and older can attend on their own, as long as they understand the proposed treatment and can give informed consent. Clients under 16 should come to their appointment with a parent or guardian.

What happens during an initial appointment?

Your initial appointment includes a review of your health history, a focused assessment, collaborative treatment planning, informed consent and hands-on treatment. These steps all take place within the time you booked.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Wear whatever is comfortable for you. You’re welcome to wear shorts during treatment, and I use draping so you stay covered at all times. Many techniques work best directly on the skin, but treatment can also be done over clothing or over the sheet if you prefer. If you’re coming in with an injury, I’ll do an assessment, so wear comfortable clothing you can move in. Depending on your needs, I may also suggest remedial exercises. Please bring water. Before your first appointment, complete your health history in Jane; otherwise it will need to be done during your appointment, which takes time away from treatment. There’s no need to arrive early. I keep the space as scent-free as possible, so please wear little or no scented products.

Can I choose what’s treated or change the pressure?

Yes. You’re in control of your treatment. Let me know which areas you’d like me to focus on or avoid, and speak up at any time if you’d like the pressure changed. You can also ask me to modify or stop treatment at any point.

Do you offer pregnancy massage?

Yes. I offer pregnancy massage with you lying on your side, supported by pillows. If you’re pregnant, please let me know through Jane, in your appointment note or your health history form.

Do you offer TMJ treatment?

Treatment may include work to the muscles around the jaw and temporomandibular joint (TMJ) when it is appropriate for your needs. I’ll explain the proposed approach and obtain your informed consent before treatment.

When can I book with you?

I’m at Gladstone on Mondays and Tuesdays from 8 a.m. to 3 p.m., Wednesdays from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Thursdays from noon to 7 p.m. I’m also in some Fridays and Sundays from 9:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. Jane shows which Fridays and Sundays I’m in, along with my current open times.

Where are you located, and what is the parking situation?

I’m at Unit 1, 481 Gladstone Avenue. The entrance is around the back of the building, so please walk around to the back rather than using the front door. Free two-hour street parking is available on Gladstone and nearby side streets, and there is a designated waiting area.

Is the location accessible?

The entrance is around the back of the building. There are steps up to the back entrance, so unfortunately there isn’t a step-free way in, and the washroom isn’t accessible either. If you have any questions about access, email me before you book and I’m happy to talk it through. My email is massage@sabrinathermt.com.

How do I book or change an appointment?

Use my secure Jane booking site to book or manage a 45-, 60- or 90-minute appointment. 30-minute appointments are available upon request only and cannot be booked online; email massage@sabrinathermt.com to request one. Visit the Services & pricing page for appointment fees. Current policies and live availability are maintained in Jane.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please give at least 48 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Cancellations made with less than 48 hours’ notice and missed appointments are charged the full appointment fee. You can cancel online through Jane. To reschedule, or if you need help rebooking, email massage@sabrinathermt.com. Email is the best way to reach me; please allow 1 to 2 business days for a reply. If you’re running late, text or call 613-801-6160.

What payment methods do you accept?

In person, I accept cash, debit and credit cards (except American Express). Online through Jane, you can pay by credit card, including American Express; debit and cash aren’t available online. American Express is accepted only through Jane, so add the card to your Jane profile to use it.

Do you offer gift cards or gift certificates?

Yes. You can buy a gift card online on Jane’s gift card page, where amounts match my appointment fees (you’ll need to sign in or create a Jane account). For a different amount, up to $500, email massage@sabrinathermt.com or ask in person.

Do you provide insurance receipts or direct billing?

I don’t direct bill insurance. Jane automatically emails you a receipt that you can submit to your insurance provider, and a printed receipt is available on request. Massage therapy coverage varies by plan, so please check with your insurance provider about your coverage and whether a doctor’s referral is required.

How can I contact you?

Email is the best way to reach me, including for booking questions, rescheduling and appointment requests: massage@sabrinathermt.com. Please allow 1 to 2 business days for a reply. If you’re running late for an appointment, text or call 613-801-6160. Please do not include personal health information in email, text messages or voicemail. Use Jane to book, cancel or manage standard appointments and to enter health-history information.

About me

My background as a competitive dancer got me interested in movement and body awareness. I tailor each treatment to your needs and may use a blend of Swedish massage, myofascial work, trigger-point techniques, joint mobilizations and stretching. Treatment may also include work to the muscles around the jaw and temporomandibular joint (TMJ), when appropriate and with your consent.

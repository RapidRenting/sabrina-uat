About Sabrina

Whether your week is spent at a desk, staying active or balancing both, Sabrina understands the importance of feeling comfortable and capable in your body. Her background as a competitive dancer inspired her interest in movement and body awareness.

Sabrina provides individualized treatments using a blend of Swedish massage, myofascial work, trigger-point techniques, joint mobilizations and stretching. Her goal is to help you understand your tension patterns, move more comfortably and continue doing the activities you enjoy. Remedial exercises may also be provided when helpful.

Treatment may also include work to the muscles around the jaw and temporomandibular joint (TMJ), when appropriate for your needs and with your consent.

Professional registration

Sabrina is registered with the College of Massage Therapists of Ontario and is currently authorized to practise. You can confirm her registration directly in the College’s public register.
